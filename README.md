# MySky

**A quiet place to let go.**

MySky is an iPhone app based on the old tradition of sky lanterns, made into a small, calming daily ritual. Pick a lantern for what you want to focus on (Health, Success, Clarity or Love), write down what's on your mind, strike a match to light it, and swipe up to let it float away into the night sky. Every lantern you release keeps glowing in your own private sky, so you can look back at what you've let go of.

Built for the Daiquiri Challenge at the Apple Developer Academy in Naples.

## How It Works

1. **Choose a lantern** by intention. Each one glows in its own color.
2. **Write** what you're holding onto.
3. **Strike a match**, light the lantern and swipe it up into the sky.

Released lanterns are saved on your device. Tap one to revisit your words, or delete it to let it go for good.

## Built With

SwiftUI, Liquid Glass, SwiftData, and custom gestures and animation (with Reduce Motion support).

## Run the Project

1. Open `MySky.xcodeproj` in Xcode 26 or later.
2. Select an iPhone simulator or device running iOS 26 or later.
3. Press **Run** (⌘R).
